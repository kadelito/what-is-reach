Welcome to the SAS Reach Program!

At SAS Reach, high school juniors step into the world of STEM careers by experiencing firsthand what it means to work, connect, and grow inside a global data analytics company.

Throughout the program, students explore the corporate world through a unique blend of professional development workshops, job shadowing, panel discussions, and mock interviews. From understanding how data shapes decisions to mastering the art of personal branding and networking, Reach participants gain the tools and insight to start building their futures today.

More than an internship, SAS Reach is an opportunity to:
  • Discover the real-world environment of STEM careers
  • Build confidence in professional communication and networking
  • Understand what companies look for in new talent
  • Connect with mentors and peers who can help open doors
    • If you're ready to take the first step toward your future, SAS Reach is where it begins.
