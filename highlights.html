Week 1

Early Career Round Table

During this session, we got to listen to some of this summer's interns talk about their jobs. Afterward, we were able to talk with them individually, asking about their jobs, any advice, and how they do certain things.

Week 2

Interview Simulation

A couple of SAS employees played the roles of interviewers and potential candidates, showing us examples of what to do and what to avoid in an interview.

Week 3

Job Shadowing

We got to spend the day with real SAS employees, witnessing a real "day in the life" and getting an in-depth look into positions at SAS.

Trip to NC State University

NC State hosted us at the Poole College of Management and College of Engineering with a full day of activities. A Shark-Tank-style "Wolf Den" teaching us about business, building a working battery and flashlight from simple materials, and, of course, Howling Cow ice cream in the Talley Student Union.

Week 4

Honey Bottling

Our group had the unique opportunity to bottle honey from SAS' own beehives. Thanks again to the environmental team for this unique education on SAS' sustainability practices!

Mock Interviews

After weeks of preparation, we took part in two rounds of mock interviews, giving us valuable insight on our own readiness and into how the interview process works.

Week 5

Intern Expo

Held at the end of the summer, the Intern Expo gave interns the opportunity to show off the work they've accomplished. Our group learned a lot about what SAS' summer interns do, as well as what we might be working on in the coming years.

SAS Reach Expo

Finally, at the end of our five weeks, we had a 2024 SAS Reach Expo of our own. We showed off our growth, what we learned, and our biggest takeaways from the program.
